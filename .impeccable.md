## Design Context

### Users
Mid-to-senior knowledge workers (engineers, PMs, AI/ML roles) running disciplined, multi-month job searches. They are technically literate — they came to career-ops via a CLI. They use the tool during focused, often-evening sessions, sometimes for hours, frequently triaging dozens of postings in a sitting. They want signal, not noise. They have already opted out of mass-application culture.

### Brand Personality
Opinionated, anti-spray-and-pray, data-forward, slightly subversive. The product's tagline — *"Companies use AI to filter candidates. I just gave candidates AI to choose companies"* — sets the tone: this is a candidate's command center, not another LinkedIn. Three words: **deliberate, instrumented, sharp**.

### Aesthetic Direction
Bauhaus / color-block editorial. Bone-cream surfaces, deep ink, and **two structural color-blocks** — vermillion (the "now" color) and navy (the structural color) — used as deliberate, separate fields rather than scattered accents. Lineage: Pitch · Are.na · It's Nice That · the considered side of Notion / Ramp. Strong grotesque display for hierarchy, refined sans for body, monospace reserved for numerical data only. System-following light + dark theme; light-default. Dense, asymmetric, table-first. The CLI/terminal heritage of the product should be visible in *information density and keyboard-first behavior*, not in chrome.

**Anti-references:** generic admin dashboards (Material/AntD), Linear-clones (clean is fine; cold-blue-violet is not us), Notion-grey, anything with a sidebar of icons-and-text-on-hover, anything with a hero card grid of "features." Also explicitly not us: the prior dark-warm-amber palette, which read as terminal cosplay.

### Design Principles
1. **Data dignifies the user.** Numbers, scores, and statuses get the most typographic weight. Chrome shrinks.
2. **Two inks, used as blocks.** Vermillion = the "now" color (live indicators, primary actions, active tab, focus ring, brand mark). Navy = the structural color (NavRail backing, header rules, hierarchical labels, secondary buttons). They never appear together as a gradient or rainbow — only as separate, deliberate fields. Status colors (sage / amber / oxblood) stay muted so the two ink-blocks retain power.
3. **Tables, not cards.** Pipeline-shaped data lives in dense rows with monospace alignment. Cards are reserved for genuine container moments.
4. **Editorial confidence, not terminal cosplay.** Bricolage Grotesque earns its weight; JetBrains Mono is reserved for numbers. Decorative CRT cues, glow, and glassmorphism are off-limits.
5. **The user is operating, not browsing.** Keyboard-first affordances are visible at all times (command bar at bottom).
